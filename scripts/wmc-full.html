      Customize your watch

      Create the watch of your dreams online with our 100% online personalized watch configurator


      Welcome to the world of ultimate personalization: WatchModCustom invites you to become the creator of your own watch, an accessory that will perfectly reflect your style and preferences.

      Thanks to our brand-new visual configurator, you can personalize your watch online in just a few minutes. Create a one-of-a-kind personalized watch with your imagination as the only limit.

      The art of personalization at your fingertips

      Case: Choose from a wide range of case models, each offering its own uniqueness.
      Dial: From the most classic to the most contemporary, from the most sober to the most flamboyant, choose the dial of your watch.
      Hands: Choose from a trio of hands to capture every precious moment of your day.
      Strap: Noble materials, refined finishes, pair your watch with a strap that perfectly matches its design. Steel or rubber, choose from a wide range of straps and colors.

      Thanks to these different options, you can create hundreds of combinations to obtain a customized watch, crafted in top-quality materials.

      Delivery times

      At WATCHMODCUSTOM, each watch is assembled on demand, guaranteeing exceptional quality. This meticulous process involves several key steps, such as rigorous water-resistance testing and precise movement adjustment with chrono comparator. Because of this meticulous attention to detail, it takes 2-3 weeks to prepare your personalized watch. We ensure that each watch meets our high standards of quality and performance, guaranteeing that your watch will be designed with the utmost care.

      High-precision automatic movement

      Our bespoke watches are equipped with a Seiko GMT NH35 automatic mechanical movement, tuned by us with 41 hours of power reserve at full load. A reliable and robust movement based on the sturdy 7s26 caliber.
      This is an automatic mechanical movement, which means that it winds itself thanks to the movement of the wearer's wrist. It can also be wound manually using the crown.
      If you opt for GMT personalization, your automatic watch will be delivered with a similar NH34 movement in architecture, also offering 41 hours of reserve.

      Sapphire crystal

      Your personalized watch will be fitted with sapphire crystal, widely recognized in the watch industry for its exceptional qualities, often considered the best choice for protecting watch dials.
      Sapphire crystal is highly resistant to scratches, as it is virtually impossible to scratch, except with a diamond or similarly hard material. Sapphire also offers excellent transparency, allowing a clear view of the dial without distortion.

      Although sapphire is more fragile than mineral glass or plastic, it offers very good shock resistance under normal conditions of watch

      Quality and authenticity

      At WatchModCustom, we don't compromise on quality. Every component we offer has been rigorously selected for its durability, beauty and finesse. We guarantee you a customized watch that will stand the test of time.

      Worldwide delivery

      We're proud to offer worldwide delivery, ensuring that your personalized watch arrives safely on your wrist.

      We pay particular attention to the packaging of each watch. Each piece is carefully wrapped in protective, shock-resistant material, ensuring its safety and integrity during transport. What's more, our delivery boxes are specially designed to offer extra protection against the hazards of transport. We are committed to ensuring that your watch arrives in the same impeccable condition as it left our workshop.
